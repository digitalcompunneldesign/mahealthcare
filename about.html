About MA Health

A public-service directory that makes Massachusetts healthcare information easier to search and discover.

Healthcare information, made easier to find

MA Health is a free, public-service directory for Massachusetts residents. We bring together information about local clinics, doctors, and common health conditions in one place, so that finding care starts with a simple search instead of a dozen open tabs.

Everything listed here is drawn from information providers share publicly — clinic websites, doctor profiles, and official contact details. We organize it, keep it readable, and connect you directly to the clinic of your choice. Booking requests go straight to the provider; we never sit between you and your care team.

What we are not: MA Health does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and we are not affiliated with the clinics listed. Always consult a licensed clinician about your health. If you are experiencing an emergency, call 911.

This project is run as a kind-cause initiative: no fees for patients, no ads between you and a doctor. If something looks out of date, write to us and we'll fix it.

Free to use

No charges, no sign-up. You pay only your clinic, directly.

Publicly sourced

Details come from providers' own published information.

Made for MA

Focused on Massachusetts communities, from Boston to Springfield.
